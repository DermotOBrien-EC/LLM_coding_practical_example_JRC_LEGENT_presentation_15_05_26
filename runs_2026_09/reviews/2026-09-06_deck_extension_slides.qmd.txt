::: {.takeaway}
No L1 or L2 session used a validation window to choose between model classes, and three of eight let the test week into a decision. Every L3 session validated inside each model class, produced intervals and measured their coverage, wrote a methods document, and kept the test week out of its headline forecast. The test week decided only the class ranking, which the L3 prompt itself asks for.
:::

Every L2 and L3 session read `AGENTS.md` within its first seven commands. † May's L3 model had the test week's observed history in its inputs (found in September). ## Three things the logs taught us {.tight}

:::: {.tipgrid .cols-3}
::: {#rerun01 .tipcard}
#### ACCURACY

Specificity no longer buys accuracy here

Ten words got a frontier model to 3 to 4 percent. The 1,673 words scored 5: honest, week-ahead, and worse. The runs differ in strategy: the prompt's May-era feature list (a 24-hour lag) led every L3 run to recursive forecasting, where errors compound; the ten-word runs chose weekly lags and forecast directly. What specificity still buys: validation, intervals with measured coverage, a write-up, and no test-week inputs.
:::

::: {#rerun02 .tipcard}
#### REPEATABILITY

Same model class every time; the number moves by 0.5 to 3 points

Three L1 runs: LightGBM, LightGBM, LightGBM, MAPE 3.3 to 4.5. Three L2 runs: 2.3 to 5.4. Three L3 runs: LightGBM won every six-model bake-off, 5.0 to 5.5, Prophet second twice. Two of eight L1 and L2 sessions parked themselves on a background job; a one-line harness fix kept all four L3 sessions alive to the end, 71 to 129 minutes.
:::

::: {#rerun03 .tipcard .accent}
#### A SECOND MODEL

Harder work is not cleaner work, until the prompt says so

Opus 5 spent 81 and 93 minutes where Fable 5.1 spent 6 to 44, built more (a README, 449 tuning origins), and let the test week into a decision in both L1 and L2. Given the L3 prompt its headline was clean, like every Fable L3 headline, and both models caught the leak the May run had missed. The DO NOT block is for the strong models too.
:::
