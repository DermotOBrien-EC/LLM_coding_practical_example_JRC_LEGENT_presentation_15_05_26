## Three things the logs taught us {.tight}

:::: {.tipgrid .cols-3}
::: {#rerun01 .tipcard}
#### ACCURACY

Specificity no longer buys accuracy here

Ten words got a frontier model to 3 to 4 percent. The 1,673 words scored 5: honest, week-ahead, and worse, because the prompt's May-era feature list (a 24-hour lag) forces error-compounding recursion that the ten-word runs avoided by choosing weekly lags on their own. What specificity still buys: validation, calibration, a write-up, and no peeking.
:::

::: {#rerun02 .tipcard}
#### REPEATABILITY

Same model class every time; the number moves by 0.5 to 3 points

Three L1 runs: LightGBM, LightGBM, LightGBM, MAPE 3.3 to 4.5. Three L2 runs: 2.3 to 5.4. Three L3 runs: LightGBM won every six-model bake-off, 5.0 to 5.5, Prophet second twice. Two of eight L1 and L2 sessions parked themselves on a background job; a one-line harness fix kept all four L3 sessions alive for 97 to 129 minutes.
:::

::: {#rerun03 .tipcard .accent}
#### A SECOND MODEL

Harder work is not cleaner work, until the prompt says so

Opus 5 spent 81 and 93 minutes where Fable 5.1 spent 6 to 44, built more (a README, 449 tuning origins), and let the test week into a decision in both L1 and L2. Given the L3 prompt it ran clean, like every Fable L3 run, and both models caught the leak the May run had missed. The DO NOT block is for the strong models too.
